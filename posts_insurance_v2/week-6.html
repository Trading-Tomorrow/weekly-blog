Week 6

Robot is alive

1 min read · by Team

During the sixth week, we successfully established communication between our humanoid robot and ROS2. This integration allows the robot to navigate the environment, locate the target car, and autonomously capture images of it from different angles. More detailed implementation and simulation details will be documented soon.

On the multi-agent side, we made progress on the claims processing pipeline. The VisionAgent now receives inspection requests and runs YOLO-based damage detection, passing results to the FraudAgent for LLM analysis. Depending on the fraud score, cases are either escalated to a HumanReviewAgent for analyst decision, or forwarded directly to the AuctioneerAgent, which runs a repair auction among available workshop agents.

On the team side, we still have colleagues missing, which has continued to impact our project dynamics and required the active team members to stretch our efforts to keep the development on track.

Week 4

State of the art delivery

1 min read · by Team

During the fourth week of our project, we focused on researching and writing the State of the Art section. This was a critical step as we needed to deliver this initial portion of our research article.

  • Literature Review: Investigated existing research in humanoid robotics, image analysis for vehicle repair, and concurrent multi-agent coordination.
  • Article Writing: Structured and drafted the State of the Art section, aligning our proposed methodologies with existing scientific literature.
  • Simulation Platform Pivot: Faced critical setup and dependency installation errors when configuring the humanoid robot in MuJoCo. As a result, we decided to pivot our robotics simulation framework from MuJoCo to Webots to ensure a smoother model setup.
This milestone establishes the theoretical foundation and the revised simulation pipeline for our upcoming implementation and prototyping phases.
